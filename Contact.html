ENGAGEMENT FAQ

Contact & Retainer Inquiries

Answers to frequent operational questions regarding consultation scheduling, response turnarounds, and confidential briefs.

What is your typical turnaround time for preliminary inquiries?

In our operational practice, inquiries received during regular business hours undergo preliminary conflict screening and receive a partner response within four business hours.

Are initial consultation briefs held in confidence?

Yes. All introductory information provided to our consulting panel is treated with strict professional confidentiality protocols, subject to conflict checks.

Can our enterprise retain Stackly on an ongoing monthly basis?

We offer structured general counsel retainers tailored for mid-market enterprises and growth companies requiring proactive, predictable corporate legal and management advisory.

How are legal advisory fees and commercial retainers structured?

We offer three engagement models: milestone-based fixed fees for defined corporate transactions (such as M&A due diligence or contract overhauls), structured monthly retainers for ongoing general counsel support, and capped hourly rates for regulatory representation. Fee schedules are delivered transparently after conflict clearance.

Does Stackly coordinate multi-jurisdiction counsel for overseas matters?

Yes. Through our international correspondent network across Singapore, the United Kingdom, and the United States, our practice partners lead cross-border matters while managing vetted local counsel for court filings and regional regulatory filings.

How does the firm manage emergency injunctions or dispute escalations?

Enterprise retainer clients have 24/7 priority access to our dispute resolution partners. In critical scenarios�such as urgent interim relief applications, freezing orders, or hostile shareholder maneuvers�a dedicated taskforce is mobilized within four hours.

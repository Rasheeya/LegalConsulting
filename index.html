FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Legal & Advisory Inquiries

Common questions regarding our consultative process, corporate advisory scope, and consultation scheduling.

How can I book a legal consultation?

You can easily request a consultation through our online portal by clicking "Book Consultation" anywhere on the site, or by visiting our Contact page. Select your practice area, preferred date, and time slot to register your demo appointment.

What documents should I bring to our initial meeting?

For corporate inquiries, please bring corporate formation documents (Articles of Association, bylaws), existing contractual drafts under review, and any relevant correspondence. For dispute assessments, bring chronological records of pertinent transactions.

Do you provide corporate legal advisory?

Yes. Our practice advises boards of directors, executive management, and shareholders on corporate governance, fiduciary obligations, mergers and acquisitions, capital structure realignment, and ongoing compliance requirements.

Can I schedule an online consultation?

Absolutely. We conduct secure virtual briefing sessions for regional and international clients across global time zones via encrypted enterprise video conferencing.

How does contract review work?

Our contract review process entails a clause-by-clause analysis to identify asymmetric risk, unmitigated liabilities, ambiguous covenants, and statutory conflicts. We return a redlined draft along with an executive advisory memorandum.

Do you assist with company registration?

Yes. We provide strategic entity selection guidance, draft shareholder pacts, prepare statutory incorporation documents, and guide founders through official regulatory filings for new commercial vehicles.

How can I check my consultation status?

Clients can sign in to the Client Dashboard using their credentials to view upcoming consultations, check real-time status updates (Pending, Confirmed, Completed), reschedule dates, or download relevant briefing notes.

How can I contact the consulting team?

You can reach our senior consulting desk by completing the inquiry form on our Contact page, emailing our advisory dispatch at info@stackly.com, or calling our corporate desk at 1800789456 during office hours.
